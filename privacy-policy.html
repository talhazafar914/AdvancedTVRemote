1. Information We Collect

Smart Remote for Roku is a utility app and does not require you to create an account to use core features. Depending on how the app is configured, the following information may be collected:

  • Basic device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Local network information needed to discover and connect to Roku devices available on your Wi-Fi network.
  • App diagnostics such as crash logs or performance data, if enabled through third-party tools.
  • Advertising identifiers or related data, if the app displays ads.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only for purposes related to operating and improving Smart Remote for Roku, including:

  • Discover Roku devices connected to the same local network.
  • Allow you to control Roku devices through the app.
  • Maintain app stability, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Support advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention if such services are enabled.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Smart Remote for Roku may use third-party services such as advertising or analytics providers. If these services are enabled, they may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Advertising providers may collect advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Analytics or crash reporting providers may collect app usage data, diagnostics, and device information to help improve reliability.

If you do not want personalized advertising, you may be able to limit ad tracking through your device settings.

4. Local Network Access

Smart Remote for Roku requests local network access so it can find and communicate with compatible Roku devices on your Wi-Fi network. Without this permission, core remote-control and device-discovery features may not work.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that support the app, such as analytics, crash reporting, or advertising partners, only as needed for those services to function.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the app, maintain service quality, comply with legal obligations, or support third-party services used by the app.

7. Your Choices

You may control data collection and permissions by:

  • Revoking local network permission in your device settings.
  • Limiting ad tracking or resetting your advertising identifier through your device settings.
  • Stopping use of the app and uninstalling it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.
